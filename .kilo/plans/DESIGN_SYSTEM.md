# DESIGN SYSTEM

## Style
Modern Financial Dashboard + Professional Trading Terminal + subtle cyberpunk. Dark, modern, not cluttered, readable for long sessions.

## Colors
Background #080A0F; Card #11151D; Secondary #171D27; Text #E5E7EB; Secondary text #929BAA; Positive #00D995; Negative #FF496C; Info #35C9FF; Analytics #A78BFA.

## Rules
- Limited glow; no excessive animation; good contrast; readable font sizes; enough whitespace; responsive; consistent color meaning.

## Info levels
Level 1: summary. Level 2: details + charts. Level 3: full analysis, history, raw data.

## Pages
Dashboard, Traders, Wallets, Signals, Analytics, Portfolio, Data Sources, Settings (see master plan section 7.3).

## Personalization
Drag cards, hide sections, watchlists, wallet groups, saved filters, display prefs.

## i18n
English + Persian from Phase 1: RTL/LTR, Persian numerals, Shamsi dates, number/currency formatting.
